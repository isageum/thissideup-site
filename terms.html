What the app does

This Side Up walks you through photographing a package (before you ship it, or right after one arrives), keeps those photos together as one record, counts down the carrier's filing window, and builds a PDF or ZIP you can send when you need to file a claim.

What it does not do. The app does not file claims, does not contact carriers, and does not send anything to your buyer. It has no way to make a carrier accept a claim, and nothing here is legal advice.
